## 2025-02-18 - Accessibility for External Links
**Learning:** External links opening in a new tab (`target="_blank"`) require `rel="noopener noreferrer"` and descriptive `aria-label`s to inform screen readers that navigation will open a new window or tab.
**Action:** Always add explicit `aria-label="[Text] (opens in a new tab)"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"` to external links.
